Atticus

Beta

Plan changes across all your repos with AI you can actually check.

Atticus has your AI assistant map how your services fit together: every API, every call between them, every database. Each fact is checked against the exact line of code it came from, and anything that doesn't match is thrown out. It also indexes your team's wikis and docs and tags each standard, how-to and decision to the stories and tasks it applies to. Then it turns a feature idea into stories and tasks your team can build, with the right knowledge attached, a built-in critic and your sign-off at every step.

Spec-driven development, built on OpenSpec: the spec is agreed before the code. Free and open source, in VS Code and IntelliJ, with GitHub Copilot or Claude Code.
